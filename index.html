THE CHALLENGE

Educational decisions need
better evidence.

Schools work with valuable educational data, but it often lives in separate documents and systems. Connecting the dots is still difficult.

01 / MATERIALS

Choosing the right textbooks

Comparing textbooks objectively is difficult without a consistent way to evaluate coverage, depth and alignment.

02 / CURRICULUM

Designing coherent programs

It takes time to understand where curriculum requirements and the learning materials do not fully align.

03 / OUTCOMES

Understanding learning gaps

Assessment scores alone may not show which specific concepts or learning objectives students find challenging.

WHAT THE PLATFORM ENABLES

From scattered information
to actionable insight.

01 — COMPARE

Textbook intelligence

Understand how well each textbook supports the curriculum — beyond chapter titles and keyword matching.

  • Topic and learning objective coverage
  • Depth of explanations and concept progression
  • Practice quality and assessment alignment
  • Transparent, evidence-backed comparison

Assessment intelligence

  • Recurring errors and challenging topics
